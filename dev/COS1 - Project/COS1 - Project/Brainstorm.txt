Turn-Based RPG Design Planning: "The Relic Switcher" 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Core Systems & Design Rules
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Weapon Sets & Quick Swapping:
    Equip two active weapon slots (Primary and Secondary). Players can swap between them mid-combat.

• Cooldown Preservation: *** Come Back To Later ***
    Skill cooldowns continue ticking down on stowed off-pocket weapons, allowing setup and execution combos across weapon swaps.

• Swap Cost:
    Swapping consumes a small amount of Stamina (e.g., 10 ST) to maintain combat pacing without infinite free swaps.

• Status Effect Build-Up:
    - Bleed: Fills a target's Bleed meter. Upon hitting 100%, deals massive flat % max-HP damage and resets.
    - Frostbite: Reduces target's Stamina regeneration per turn by 50% for 3 turns once triggered.
    - Burn (DoT): Deals ticking elemental damage over consecutive turn resolution phases.

• Telegraphed Intent System:
    - Preparation Phase (Turn Start): Boss announces intent (e.g., Winding up Heavy Cleave).
    - Player Action Phase: Player executes attacks, casts skills, blocks, or dodges.
    - Resolution Phase (Turn End): Player actions resolve followed by the boss's telegraphed intent.

• Core Stats:
    - HP: Health Points
    - SP: Stamina Points
    - MP: Mana Points

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Core Attributes & Scaling Rules
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Constitution (CON)
    - Base (1): Provides 80 HP and baseline physical/status resistance.
    - Primary: Increases Max HP (+10 HP per point).
    - Secondary: Increases Physical Resistance & Status Buildup Resistance.

• Strength (STR)
    - Base (1): Base heavy weapon damage and base posture damage.
    - Primary: Boosts damage with heavier weapons.
    - Secondary: Increases Posture/Stamina drain on hit; reduces posture drain when blocking.

• Agility (AGI)
    - Base (1): Provides 50 SP and baseline Stamina regen per turn.
    - Primary: Boosts damage with light weapons.
    - Secondary: Increases Max Stamina (+5 SP per point), dodge chance, and multi-hit potential.

• Intellect (INT)
    - Base (1): Provides 50 MP and base magic damage.
    - Primary: Boosts Magic and Status Damage.
    - Secondary: Increases Max Mana (+10 MP per point).

• Focus (FOC)
    - Base (1): Base support/healing scaling and standard cooldown rates.
    - Primary: Boosts Healing power.
    - Secondary: Reduces turn cooldowns for skills across equipped weapons.

• Character Allocation: Each attribute starts at 1. The player receives 10 starting points to allocate freely (Total: 15 stat points at start).

3. Weapon Roster & Resource Identities 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Pure STR Weapons]
• Warhammer : Slower attack speed with high physical damage and massive stamina/posture damage. Ideal for causing staggers. 
    - Scaling: 100% STR 
    - Cost: 
        Attack: Very High SP (cost lowered with higher STR)
        Block: N/A
        Dodge: Very High SP

• Sword and Shield : Balanced offensive/defensive weapon with high block stability and counter-attack potential. 
    - Scaling: 100% STR
    - Cost: 
        Attack: Low SP (lowered with higher STR) 
        Block: Moderate SP (lowered with higher STR)
        Dodge: Executes a Parry for High SP (lowered with higher STR)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Pure AGI Weapons]
• Daggers : Faster attack speed allowing multi-hits in a single turn. High critical hit chance and rapid Bleed buildup.
    - Scaling: 100% AGI
    - Cost:
        Attack: Moderate SP (lower cost, higher multi-hit chance, and faster buildup with higher AGI)
        Block: Low SP (Parry stance)
        Dodge: Low SP (lowered with higher AGI)

• Bow : Ranged physical damage. Capable of quick shots or sacrificing turns to aim for massive damage.
    - Scaling: 100% AGI
    - Cost:
        Attack: Low SP (higher AGI adds dodge chance while aiming and increases aim damage multiplier)
        Block: N/A
        Dodge: Low SP (lowered with higher AGI)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Pure INT Weapons]
• Fire Staff : High magic damage with Burn status buildup that causes damage over time.
    - Scaling: 100% INT
    - Cost:
        Attack: High MP (lower cost and faster status buildup with higher INT)
        Block: High MP (Mana Barrier)
        Dodge: Moderate SP

• Ice Staff : Control and mitigation. Reduces enemy stamina regeneration and absorbs incoming damage.
    - Scaling: 100% INT
    - Cost:
        Attack: Moderate MP (lower cost and higher stamina drain on hit with higher INT)
        Block: Moderate MP (Ice Wall / Block potential increased with higher INT)
        Dodge: Moderate SP

[Pure FOC Weapons]
• Life Staff : Essential sustain. Provides self-healing and reduces skill cooldowns across equipped weapons.
    - Scaling: 100% FOC
    - Cost:
        Attack: Moderate MP (lower cost, higher healing, and greater cooldown reduction with higher FOC)
        Block: Moderate MP (Ward Shield)
        Dodge: Moderate SP
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Hybrid Weapons]
• Greatsword : Mixed physical damage capable of light or heavy attacks. Heavy attacks scale off STR; Light attacks multi-hit scaling off AGI.
    - Scaling: 50% STR / 50% AGI
    - Cost:
        Attack (Heavy): High SP (lowered with higher STR)
        Attack (Light): Moderate SP (lowered with higher AGI, increased multi-hit chance)
        Block: Moderate SP (Stance Block)
        Dodge: Moderate SP

• Rapier : Finesse weapon blending light physical thrusts with cross-resource detonation (Bleed applied by AGI is detonated with MP; Burn applied by INT is detonated with SP).
    - Scaling: 50% AGI / 50% INT
    - Cost:
        Attack (Light): Very Low SP
        Detonate Bleed: Moderate MP (lowered with higher AGI)
        Detonate Burn: Moderate SP (lowered with higher INT)
        Block: Low SP (Parry)
        Dodge: Low SP (lowered with higher AGI)

• Flail and Shield : Paladin drain-tanking. Low attack, moderate block, low stamina damage, with a small self-heal on attack or block (requires FOC > 2).
    - Scaling: 50% STR / 50% FOC
    - Cost:
        Attack: Low SP (damage/posture drain increased with higher STR; heal amount increased with higher FOC)
        Block: Moderate SP (lowered with higher STR; heal amount increased with higher FOC)
